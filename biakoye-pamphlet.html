Understanding the plan

Our 6 Super Powers

Biakoye's leaders are focusing on six priority areas — every one points back at the same thing: every child in Biakoye.

Happy Homes & Caring Families

Positive parenting

A happy home is where every child's story begins!

  • Parenting classes — teaching mums, dads and caregivers how to give positive love, care and support.
  • Family support groups — families share ideas, help one another and learn together.
  • Community champions — chiefs, religious leaders and local heroes who speak up for children.
2

Strong Bodies & Healthy Tummies

Nutrition and health

Growing bodies need good food, clean hands and a quick check-up!

  • Home gardens — families grow fresh, tasty fruits and vegetables right at home.
  • Health check-ups — checking on little ones early, so anyone growing slowly gets extra help right away.
  • Clean hands & safe boats — handwashing stations at schools, and boats that carry health workers and medicine to children on islands.
Inside 1

Safe Communities & Protective Shields

Child protection

A whole community standing guard, like a giant shield around every child!

  • Home visits — caring helpers visit homes to make sure every child is safe from harm.
  • Anti-drug clubs in schools — helping older kids make smart, healthy choices.
  • Speaking up — easy ways for anyone to tell someone when a child needs help.
4

Welcoming Spaces for Everyone

Disability inclusion

Every door open, every path smooth — so every child can join in!

  • Checking our buildings — schools, health centres and playgrounds easy for everyone to get into.
  • Ramps & friendly doors — ramps, smooth paths and easy doors for children who use wheelchairs.
  • Fun playgrounds for all — parks, daycares and libraries with clear signs, pictures and sounds.
5

Super Teachers & Great Learning

Early learning

Great teachers and fun tools make learning an adventure!

  • Trained teachers & caregivers — special training to support every child's learning.
  • Fun learning tools — fun books, toys and activity guides in local languages.
  • Checking quality — looking at schools again and again, so learning stays fun, safe and helpful.
